📡 Train GPS & Tunnel Awareness

This page demonstrates a moving train feed and what happens when GPS is unavailable inside a tunnel.

How this works

During a tunnel segment, GPS is intentionally marked unavailable. The system keeps the last known fix and estimates movement from route data until GPS becomes available again. This is a working simulation; real train GPS requires an onboard GPS device or railway tracking API.
